// why these exist

Why I made these.

Something to stare at

A minute of planking feels endless when you're staring at the floor, so I made something to stare at instead: Plank Pilot. Game Life is the same idea, a screen that keeps changing all by itself.

Someone to play with

A few of these I made to play with girls I liked. They ghosted me; the games stayed. Now you can play them with someone who won't.

A reason to actually read

My professor told me to stop letting AI summarize papers and actually read them. Social media had worn my focus thin, and no reader I tried kept me on the page, so I built Phloem.

Plank Pilot

Plank timer games, hands-free

  • iPhone · iPad
  • Free
  • In beta testing

Hold a plank and play with your face, so your hands stay planted and the clock stops dragging. Two games: a flight through a meteor belt, and a sword duel on a plank.

  • Flight: dodge fiery meteors, grab stars and ? boxes, and survive the double-points final stretch.
  • Plank Plank Plank!: strike and block against a goat, a lava golem, a yeti, a crab and a robot, or duel a friend over FaceTime.
  • Play with small face shifts, your eyebrows, or a nod and a shake. Face tracking never leaves your iPhone.

Game Life: Cellular Worlds

Conway's automaton, reimagined

  • iPhone · iPad
  • Free
  • In App Review

A tiny universe that runs on three simple rules. Draw a few cells, press Play and watch them live, die and multiply.

  • See what's coming: cells coloured by their fate, and Lookahead up to 55 generations ahead.
  • Eleven puzzles, from surviving 20 generations to outlasting a shrinking arena.
  • Music Box turns the board into a melody. Draw with a finger or Apple Pencil.

Qi: Go & Gomoku

Learn Go and five in a row

  • iPhone · iPad
  • Free
  • In App Review

Two of the oldest board games, made for touch and for learning.

  • Go on 9×9, 13×13 or 19×19 against a patient beginner opponent, or with a friend on one device.
  • A coach rings the most urgent move and explains why in one sentence.
  • Gomoku against Easy, Normal or Hard. In English and 中文.

Manilha

Bid your tricks, mind the trump

  • iPhone · iPad
  • Free
  • In App Review

The Brazilian trick-bidding card game also known as Fodinha, or Oh Hell. Call how many tricks you'll take, then take exactly that many.

  • A new manilha every round: one rank beats every other card.
  • Blind rounds: you see everyone's card except your own.
  • Solo against up to three computers, or two of you on one iPhone or iPad. English, Português, Español and 中文.

Phloem: Paper Reader

A calm reading desk for research papers

  • iPad
  • Free
  • Available now

A calm, local-first reading desk for research papers on iPad. Import PDFs and Word files, read without distractions, and keep your thinking beside the source.

  • A reading guide that walks you down the page line by line, in light or dark.
  • Ask AI to explain any passage: select it and get a plain explanation, marked for you to verify.
  • Book view with real page turns, and Apple Pencil notes right on the page in eight inks.
